# CAZI-MBN
Multiplex Biological Networks (MBNs), which represent multiple interaction types between entities, are crucial for understanding complex biological systems. We propose a novel framework for zero-shot interaction prediction in MBNs by leveraging context-aware representation learning and knowledge distillation. Our approach leverages domain-specific foundation models to generate enriched embeddings, introduces a topology-aware graph tokenizer to capture multiplexity and higher-order connectivity, and employs contrastive learning to align embeddings across modalities. A teacher–student distillation strategy further enables robust zero-shot generalization. Experimental results demonstrate that our framework outperforms state-of-the-art methods in interaction prediction for MBNs, providing a powerful tool for exploring various biological interactions and advancing personalized therapeutics.
